
# Maven Fuzzy Factory SQL Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes an e-commerce database using MySQL to evaluate website performance, marketing effectiveness, product performance, customer behavior, and overall business profitability. The analysis focuses on solving real-world business problems using SQL and generating actionable insights for decision-making.

## Tools Used

- MySQL
- MySQL Workbench

## Database Tables

- website_sessions
- website_pageviews
- orders
- order_items
- products
- order_item_refunds

## Business Problems Solved

- Website Performance Analysis
- Marketing Performance Analysis
- Revenue and Profit Analysis
- Product Performance Analysis
- Refund Analysis
- Customer Behavior Analysis
- Device Performance Analysis
- Website Traffic Analysis

## Key Business Insights

### Website Performance

- Total Website Sessions: **38,719**
- Total Orders: **1,198**
- Website Conversion Rate: **3.09%**
- Total Revenue: **$628,722.89**
- Total Profit: **$387,257.50**

### Marketing Performance

- GSearch was the primary acquisition channel, generating **31,347 sessions**, **1,127 orders**, and **$56,338** in revenue.
- BSearch generated **4,105 sessions**, **192 orders**, and **$9,598** in revenue.
- Direct Traffic contributed **3,267 sessions**, **156 orders**, and **$7,798** in revenue.
- GSearch proved to be the most effective marketing channel in terms of traffic, conversions, and revenue generation.

### Product Performance

- The Original Mr. Fuzzy generated the highest revenue (**$627,874.40**) and recorded the highest number of units sold (**12,560**).
- The Birthday Sugar Panda generated **$60,016.95** in revenue.
- The Hudson River Mini Bear generated **$27,200.93** in revenue.
- Revenue distribution indicates a strong dependence on The Original Mr. Fuzzy.

### Refund Analysis

- The Birthday Sugar Panda had the highest refund rate (**6.67%**).
- The Original Mr. Fuzzy recorded a refund rate of **4.82%**.
- The Hudson River Mini Bear had the lowest refund rate (**0.99%**).

### Customer Behavior

- Approximately **91%** of website sessions came from new visitors.
- Approximately **9%** of sessions were from repeat visitors.
- Customer acquisition is strong, while customer retention presents an opportunity for improvement.

### Device Performance

- Desktop conversion rate: **4.72%**
- Mobile conversion rate: **1.32%**
- Desktop users converted at a significantly higher rate than mobile users.

### Website Traffic Trends

- Website traffic peaked between **10:00 AM and 4:00 PM**.
- Friday recorded the highest number of orders.
- Saturday and Sunday recorded the lowest number of orders.

## Business Recommendations

- Continue investing in GSearch as the primary customer acquisition channel.
- Improve the mobile website experience to increase mobile conversion rates.
- Investigate the higher refund rate associated with The Birthday Sugar Panda.
- Implement customer retention strategies to increase repeat purchases.
- Reduce dependence on The Original Mr. Fuzzy by promoting other product lines.
- Schedule marketing campaigns during peak traffic hours.
- Introduce promotional campaigns during weekends to improve weekend sales.
